# Partnerships

Digital Insight AI is open to collaboration with:

- Saudi SMEs and e-commerce teams willing to validate a real workflow,
- automation and software vendors that support reversible, auditable implementations,
- security and privacy specialists,
- implementation partners,
- research and ecosystem organizations.

## Partnership principles

1. Real workflow before tool selection.
2. Baseline before ROI claims.
3. Human approval for material decisions.
4. Minimum necessary data access.
5. Reversible pilots before scale.
6. No fabricated case studies.
7. No hidden affiliate influence on recommendations.
