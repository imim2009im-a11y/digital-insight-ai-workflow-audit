# Digital Insight AI — AI Workflow Audit

A public, evidence-first showcase of the AI Workflow Audit methodology used by Digital Insight AI.

## Current status

**Validation only. No paid checkout is enabled.**

This repository exists to show companies, partners, and technical collaborators how the service is designed, tested, and governed before any paid engagement is offered.

## What the audit does

The audit focuses on one measurable workflow at a time:

1. document the current process,
2. establish a baseline,
3. identify repetitive work and bottlenecks,
4. rank automation candidates by impact, reversibility, privacy, and implementation risk,
5. define a small controlled pilot,
6. measure the result against the baseline.

It does not promise revenue, savings, or automatic replacement of employees.

## What this repository proves

- the service has concrete deliverables,
- the methodology can be reviewed publicly,
- claims are constrained by evidence,
- paid-sales readiness is blocked until compliance requirements are satisfied,
- secrets and client data are not part of the public repository.
