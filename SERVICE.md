# Service Method

## Scope

One workflow per audit.

Examples:
- repetitive reporting,
- product/content operations,
- partner onboarding,
- customer-support triage,
- manual data transfer,
- recurring internal coordination.

## Deliverables

- current-state workflow map,
- measurable baseline,
- 3–5 ranked opportunities,
- risk/privacy notes,
- recommended controlled pilot,
- 30-day implementation roadmap,
- success metrics and rollback plan.

## Evidence rule

No ROI, savings, or revenue percentage is published unless measured against a documented baseline.

## Current commercial status

This public showcase is in market-validation mode.

- no public checkout,
- no payment collection,
- no guaranteed outcome claims,
- no paid work begins before legal/commercial readiness and written scope are confirmed.
