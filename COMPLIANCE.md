# Commercial Readiness Status

This repository is not evidence that Digital Insight AI is legally ready to accept paid work.

Paid sales remain blocked until applicable requirements are evidenced, including:
- valid legal operating basis for the activity,
- compliant service terms,
- privacy and data-processing disclosures,
- invoicing and tax status,
- advertising and outreach requirements,
- provider identity and contact disclosures.

The automated checks are operational guardrails, not legal advice.
